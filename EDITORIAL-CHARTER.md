# Veer Smriti — Editorial Charter

## Core principle
**No rank above remembrance. No name beyond our care.**

Veer Smriti does not rank lives by fame, rank, awards, sacrifice, readership or emotional impact. It does not have a story-count target. Stories are added when there is something meaningful and trustworthy to preserve.

## Two complementary perspectives
Where relevant and available, an individual story may contain:

1. **The documented record** — information supported by official records, credible historical sources, or other verifiable evidence.
2. **Family remembrance** — memories and perspectives shared by relatives or people who knew the individual, with appropriate consent and clear attribution.

These perspectives are complementary, not competing. The distinction tells readers how information is known; it does not decide whose voice matters more.

## Evidence and uncertainty
- Cite specific sources for factual claims.
- Distinguish documented facts from firsthand recollections, secondhand accounts, interpretation and unresolved claims.
- Investigate discrepancies respectfully and explain uncertainty plainly.
- Family testimony is valuable evidence of lived experience, but it does not automatically settle every historical or operational question.
- Missing documentation does not, by itself, prove a recollection false.
- Do not invent dialogue, private thoughts, final words, family memories or operational details.
- If a claim cannot be responsibly supported, qualify it or withhold it.

## Consent, privacy and safety
- Obtain appropriate permission for private family materials, photographs, letters and attributed testimony.
- Minimize personal data and avoid publishing private contact details or sensitive information.
- Do not publish operationally sensitive details merely because they were submitted.
- Give contributors a way to request corrections or raise concerns before the project has a public submission system.

## Equal dignity
Combat, medical, engineering, technical, communications, logistics, naval, aircrew and other service roles deserve equal editorial respect. Article length may vary with available evidence, but dignity does not.

## Corrections
Maintain source notes and revise articles when credible new information is provided. Make meaningful corrections transparently.
